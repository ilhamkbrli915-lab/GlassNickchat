Nickchat

Sign in with your Gmail and a nickname. Chat, call and video call your friends. No phone number needed.

Nickname: 3 to 20 characters, letters, digits, _ and -. Friends add you by this exact nickname. A nickname belongs to the first Gmail that uses it. Your Gmail is only stored as a scrambled code.
